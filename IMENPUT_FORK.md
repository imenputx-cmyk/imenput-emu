# imenput-emu (fork of Lichii / Eden-NX2)

This repository continues **Lichii** (https://github.com/zortos293/lichii, a Switch 2 fork of the Eden
emulator) at commit `031c8a8`. It is licensed under the GNU GPL v3 like its upstream; all original
copyright and license notices are kept. Changes made here are listed in the git history.

Rules for this repository:
- No keys, firmware, fonts or game files are ever committed. They come from your own console.
- Goal: get Switch 2 titles (first: Donkey Kong Bananza) rendering. See `NX2_NOTES.md` for the
  roadmap inherited from Lichii and `NX2_IMENPUT.md` for the notes of this fork.
